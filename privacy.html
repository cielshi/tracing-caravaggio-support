Tracing Caravaggio

Privacy Policy

Your exhibition progress stays on your device. Tracing Caravaggio requires no app account and includes no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. Apple handles purchases; saving and sharing images are your choice.

01Information in the App

We do not collect your exhibition activity, location, contacts, device identifiers, or usage analytics. We do not operate an app server that receives your progress or purchase transactions.

The app stores found clues, chapter progress, preferences, and purchase-access state locally. It uses purchase information verified through Apple’s StoreKit to determine access to the full exhibition; this information is not sent to our servers.

02Offline Exhibition and Purchases

Artwork, text, and fonts are included in the app. The exhibition works offline, including purchased content when its access is already available on your device. Loading prices, purchasing, and restoring purchases may require a connection to Apple.

Apple processes payments under its Privacy Policy. We do not receive your payment-card details or Apple Account credentials. Restarting the story does not cancel your purchase.

03Saving Images to Photos

If you choose Save Image, the app requests permission to add the selected artwork to Photos. It does not read or upload your existing photo library. You can decline this optional permission and continue using the exhibition, or manage it in iOS Settings.

04Sharing and External Services

When you share a painting postcard, iOS passes the selected image and accompanying information to the destination you choose. That service handles the content under its own privacy practices. We do not receive your messages, recipients, or sharing history.

The app has no embedded social-media, advertising, or analytics SDKs. Opening an external link or scanning a QR code can connect you to another provider, which may receive information such as your IP address.

05Contacting Support

If you email support, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments or device information you choose to provide. We use this information to respond and troubleshoot, not for advertising. Please do not send passwords or payment-card details.

Support correspondence is handled by our email provider and retained as needed to resolve your request and meet applicable obligations. You can email us to request deletion of your correspondence.

06Public Policy and Support Website

Our policy and support pages do not add advertising, analytics, or tracking cookies. The hosting provider may process IP addresses and technical logs to deliver and secure the website. The GitHub Pages version is subject to GitHub’s Privacy Statement. Reading the bundled privacy page inside the app does not contact that website.

07Children

The exhibition requires no account, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If a child sends personal information to support, a parent or guardian can contact us to request its deletion.

08Your Choices and Deleting Data

Restart resets your story progress without removing purchased access. Deleting the app removes its local data; Apple’s purchase records remain separate and eligible purchases can be restored. Device backups are managed through your Apple and iOS settings.

Saved images remain in Photos until you delete them. Shared copies are managed by their recipients or destination service. The app does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, or notification access; adding images to Photos is optional.

09Changes and Contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise its effective date. Questions or privacy requests? Email thisisneatworks@gmail.com.
