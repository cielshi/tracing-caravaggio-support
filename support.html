Frequently Asked Questions

What is included with the app?

The app is free to download, with the prologue and first chapter available to try. An optional, one-time in-app purchase unlocks the complete exhibition: 25 paintings, eight chapters, and the ending. There are no subscriptions or advertisements. The price is shown before you purchase.

How do I restore my purchase?

Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase, connect to the internet, and select Restore Purchase on the unlock screen. Restoring an eligible purchase does not charge you again. Restarting the story does not remove purchased access.

Does the exhibition work offline?

Yes. Artwork, text, and fonts are included in the app. Purchased content can be used offline when access is already available on your device. Loading prices, purchasing, restoring purchases, opening external links, or sending content through an online service may require internet access.

How is my progress saved?

Your progress is stored only on your device. Tracing Caravaggio does not require an account and does not send your progress to a server.

How do I start again?

Open the menu inside the app and choose Restart. Deleting the app also removes its locally saved progress.

I cannot download the app or have a billing question.

Purchases, downloads, and refund requests are handled by Apple. Visit Report a Problem or contact Apple Support for help with your App Store account.

How do I save or share a painting?

Find every clue in a painting to collect it. Open the painting from Collection, tap the artwork to view it full screen, then tap the share button. Sharing sends a painting postcard; Save Image saves the artwork itself to Photos.

Saving requires permission to add images to Photos, without reading your existing library. If you previously declined, you can change the permission in iOS Settings. Closing the share panel leaves you in the full-screen artwork view; tap again to return to its frame.

What information does the app collect?

The app does not collect exhibition activity or analytics. Apple handles purchases, your chosen destination handles shared content, and we receive information you choose to send by email. Read the full Privacy Policy.

Which terms apply to the app?

The app uses Apple’s Standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement.

Where do the artwork images come from?

Find the individual image sources and reproduction notices in Artwork Sources.
